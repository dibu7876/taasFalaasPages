This policy describes the information Taas Falaas processes when you use the app, including local play, online rooms, invitations and voice chat.

01

Information the app handles

Account and sign-in details

When you sign in with Apple, the app receives an Apple account identifier and may receive a display name. Apple’s sign-in credential can contain other claims, such as an email address, depending on your Apple account and sign-in settings. The app uses the credential to authenticate your sign-in. The Taas Falaas profile table does not have an email field. You can edit your display name. Guest mode creates a guest account with a generated ID and name.

The app stores session credentials in the iOS Keychain. The server stores account and session records, including Apple’s account identifier; refresh tokens are stored as hashes.

Game and room information

Online play requires the server to process room codes, game type and settings, player names and seats, cards and game state, results, and virtual credit balances or transfers. Other players in your room can see the game information needed to play, such as player names, cards and moves, and match results.

The app may also save local preferences, including Marriage hand order and pending invitation information.

Invitations and notifications

The server stores invitation details such as sender, recipient, room code and read status. If push notifications are enabled, the server stores the device push token and its Apple push environment to deliver notifications.

Voice chat

If you join voice chat and grant microphone permission, audio is sent in short packets through the game server to players subscribed to voice in that room. The server documentation says audio is not stored. Voice is transmitted over a secure connection, but it is not end-to-end encrypted.

02

How information is used and shared

We use information to sign players in, operate rooms and matches, show player names and results, deliver invitations and support voice chat.

Room information is shared with the other players who need it to participate in a match. Voice audio is relayed to players subscribed in that room. Apple processes sign-in, and Apple Push Notification service may process push notifications. Taas Falaas hosting infrastructure processes the information sent to the game server. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not collect analytics or advertising tracking data. We do not record IP addresses, device details or error information in our server logs.

Virtual table credits are for in-game entertainment. They help set a fixed play time before players are removed from a multiplayer room. They are not cash betting or cash prizes.

03

Retention and account deletion

We do not currently publish fixed retention periods for account, match, invitation, push-token or server-log records. Some records may remain after account deletion. We will update this policy when retention periods are set.

The app does not currently offer a self-service account deletion option. To request account deletion, email cstorereporting-info@cstorereporting.com. The server’s deletion process revokes the Apple credential and marks the account deleted, clearing its display name; related records may remain.

04

Security and location

Session credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Voice is transmitted over a secure connection. The service is hosted in the United States.

05

Contact

Taas Falaas is the service operator. For questions about this policy or to make a privacy request, contact us at cstorereporting-info@cstorereporting.com.
